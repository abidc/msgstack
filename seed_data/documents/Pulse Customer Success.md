# Pulse Customer Success

CS platform for SaaS companies. Proactive health scores, automated engagements, and revenue retention.

## Positioning
Pulse turns customer success from reactive firefighting into proactive revenue protection. AI that identifies churn risk before it materializes.

## Target Audience
SaaS companies, 50K+ ARR

## Tagline
Retention before it's a problem.

## Differentiation
Predictive churn scoring vs NPS surveys. Automated playbook execution vs manual outreach. Revenue attribution vs activity tracking.

## Key Messages

### Headlines (Priority 1-3)
- Churn prediction that actually predicts.
- Turn reactive CS into proactive retention.
- Revenue retention at scale.

### Subheads (Priority 1-3)
- AI health scores updated daily. Not quarterly surveys.
- Automated playbooks trigger at risk threshold.
- Revenue attribution tied to every engagement.

### Benefits (Priority 1-4)
- Identify 84% of churn risk 30 days early.
- Reduce churn by 23% on average.
- Scale CS from 1:100 to 1:200 without headcount.
- Slack integration triggers outreach automatically.

- $340M ARR saved from churn across customers.
- 4.7/5 G2 from 203 CS leaders.

### Social Proof (Priority 1-3)
