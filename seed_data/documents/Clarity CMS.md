# Clarity CMS

Headless CMS for enterprise content teams. API-first, developer-friendly, marketer-approved.

## Positioning
Clarity gives developers the API flexibility they want and marketers the editing experience they love. The CMS that doesn't slow anyone down.

## Target Audience
Enterprise marketing teams, 500+ employees

## Tagline
Content without the friction.

## Differentiation
Headless-first vs add-on headless. 50+ native integrations vs requiring middleware. Real-time preview vs save-and-pray.

## Key Messages

### Headlines (Priority 1-3)
- Your content deserves better than a clunky CMS.
- API-first CMS that developers actually like.
- Content at the speed of your team.

### Subheads (Priority 1-3)
- 50+ native integrations: Next.js, Hugo, Contentful migration, and more.
